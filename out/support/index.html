Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which iPhones are supported?

A: iPhone X or later with a TrueDepth camera and iOS 17.0+ is required.

Q: Is camera footage saved?

A: No. Camera footage is processed in real time on-device only and is never saved or transmitted.

Q: Will ads disappear after purchasing PASS?

A: Yes. All ads are removed after purchasing PASS.

Q: Is PASS a subscription?

A: No. It is a one-time purchase of ¥250. Once purchased, you have lifetime access.

Q: How do I restore my purchase?

A: Tap "Restore Purchase" in the in-app settings screen.

Q: Mouth detection is not working well

A: Make sure you are in a well-lit area with your face fully visible to the camera. You can also adjust sensitivity using the calibration feature.
